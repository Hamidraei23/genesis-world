Robot controls

End-effector target

Pose of the fingertip point, midway between the finger tips, in the robot base frame. Roll, pitch and yaw turn about that point. Drag the gizmo at the gripper, jog an axis, or enter a target.

Target follows the current fingertip point.

Kinematics only; no collision checking. If a target cannot be solved, the arm stays at its last valid pose.
